Meet the Mob Bosses

Maj Mob was created by two friends who love the game, love teaching it, and love bringing people together around the table. Whether you are brand new or just need things explained in a simpler way, we are here to make learning Mah Jongg feel fun, social, and doable.

Janie & Caren, Founders of Maj Mob

Janie

About Janie

I may be newer to Mah Jongg, but that is exactly why I understand how overwhelming it can feel when you are first learning. I have been playing for about a year, and it took me three different attempts at lessons before I finally stuck with it. Once I did, I was hooked.

With a degree in Education and more than 15 years of experience teaching, I know how to break things down into simple, easy-to-follow steps. My goal is to make learning Mah Jongg feel less intimidating, more understandable, and a lot more fun.

Caren

About Caren

I’ve been playing Mah Jongg for over 50 years, inspired by my mother, who played for nearly 70 years and brought a deep passion for the game to our family. While she was a very serious player, I simply fell in love with the joy of playing.

Now, I enjoy sharing that love with others—helping new players learn the game in a fun, welcoming, and relaxed environment.

Why We Teach Together

Together, we bring the perfect combination of experience and fresh perspective. We make Mah Jongg approachable, social, and fun — whether you are brand new, coming back after years away, or just looking for a more relaxed way to learn.

Maj 101

New to Mahj? This class is the perfect place to start.

If you’ve ever looked at the National Mah Jongg League card and felt completely overwhelmed, you are not alone. We teach Mahj in a simple, friendly, non-intimidating way that makes the game much easier to understand.

In Maj 101, you’ll start with the basics: learning the tiles, the rules, and how the game works. By the end of class, you’ll be comfortable playing a modified version of Mahj, understand how the game flows, and have a solid foundation to keep learning and playing.

No experience needed — just come ready to learn and have fun.

Level 2 $50 / person

Maj 102

Ready for the next step?

Maj 102 builds on what you learned in Maj 101 and helps you move closer to playing the full game with confidence.

In this class, we will reinforce the basics, then introduce the deal, the Charleston, how to start choosing a line on the National Mah Jongg League card, basic etiquette, and simple strategies to help you begin making decisions during play.

We will use the National Mah Jongg League card in class. By the end of Maj 102, you’ll understand how the game comes together and be ready to play a full game of Mahj.

Guided Play

Looking to practice and refine your skills?

Guided Play is designed for players who know the basics of Mah Jongg (or have taken Maj 101 & 102) and want hands-on experience playing real games with an instructor present.

Play full games at a comfortable, relaxed pace while receiving real-time advice on hand selection, tile strategy, and card navigation. Ask questions as hands unfold and build confidence at the table.

All tiles, racks, and National Mah Jongg League cards are provided.
